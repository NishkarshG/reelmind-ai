EPISODE 4
LOGLINE: Maya confronts Vikram on the rooftop about the night of the fire, and learns the truth about the body he was shown.
CONTINUITY NOTES: Night. Maya now wears Meera's old shawl. The crushed locket is in her left hand throughout Scene 1. Vikram's bandage is visible for the first time.

SCENE 1 - EXT. MALHOTRA MANSION ROOFTOP - NIGHT (00:00:00 - 00:00:54)
CAMERA - Wide establishing shot of the city skyline, then a slow push toward two silhouettes.

ACTION (00:00:00 - 00:00:05)
Wind whips across the rooftop. Maya stands at the edge, holding the note from the locket.

VIKRAM (00:00:05 - 00:00:09)
You shouldn't be up here alone.

MAYA (00:00:09 - 00:00:15)
(without turning)
My mother wrote that she was afraid of Nandini. Why would she write that, Vikram?

ACTION (00:00:15 - 00:00:20)
Vikram pulls off his glove. His hand is scarred from palm to wrist.

VIKRAM (00:00:20 - 00:00:26)
I burned this trying to break that bolt. The door was chained from outside. I couldn't get to you.

MAYA (00:00:26 - 00:00:31)
Then who pulled me out? I woke up in a hospital with no name.

VIKRAM (00:00:31 - 00:00:37)
I don't know. But the next morning Nandini took me to the morgue. She said the body was yours.

ACTION (00:00:37 - 00:00:42)
Maya turns. Tears she has held back for five years finally fall.

MAYA (00:00:42 - 00:00:48)
And you believed her. You got engaged to her daughter.

VIKRAM (00:00:48 - 00:00:54)
I got engaged because Nandini owns the debt that could bury my father. I had no choice.

SCENE 2 - INT. MALHOTRA MANSION - STAIRWELL - NIGHT (00:00:54 - 00:01:40)
CAMERA - Dutch angle, descending.

ACTION (00:00:54 - 00:00:59)
Rhea hides on the stairs below, recording on her phone.

RHEA (00:00:59 - 00:01:03)
(whispering into the phone)
Mumma, you need to hear this.

ACTION (00:01:03 - 00:01:08)
The recording plays back: Vikram's voice, "Nandini took me to the morgue."

NANDINI (V.O.) (00:01:08 - 00:01:14)
Delete it. Now. And Rhea — bring him to me. Tonight.

RHEA (00:01:14 - 00:01:19)
He's my fiancé, Mumma. Whatever you did, I'm not losing him to her.

ACTION (00:01:19 - 00:01:25)
A long pause on the line. Then Nandini's voice, colder than ice.

NANDINI (V.O.) (00:01:25 - 00:01:32)
You were never going to have him. He was a contract. Now do as I say.

ACTION (00:01:32 - 00:01:40)
Rhea lowers the phone, shaking. For the first time, she looks afraid of her own mother.

SCENE 3 - EXT. MALHOTRA MANSION ROOFTOP - NIGHT (00:01:40 - 00:02:26)
CAMERA - Tight two-shot, shallow focus.

MAYA (00:01:40 - 00:01:45)
If my mother was murdered, I want the person who did it to lose everything.

VIKRAM (00:01:45 - 00:01:50)
Then we do it together. No more secrets.

ACTION (00:01:50 - 00:01:56)
They stand close. He brushes rain from her cheek. Neither moves away.

ACTION (00:01:56 - 00:02:01)
The rooftop door bangs open. Two of Nandini's security men step out.

GUARD (00:02:01 - 00:02:06)
Mr. Malhotra. Madam Nandini wants you downstairs. Now.

VIKRAM (00:02:06 - 00:02:10)
Tell her I'll come in the morning.

ACTION (00:02:10 - 00:02:16)
The guard steps aside. Behind him, Nandini herself walks onto the roof, smiling.

NANDINI (00:02:16 - 00:02:26)
Meera stood exactly where you're standing, Maya. The night before the fire. Shall I tell you what she said?
